# Rubrics

This directory will contain reusable grading rubrics for Project VITAL modules.

## General Assessment Philosophy

Students should be evaluated on the quality of their testing work, not simply on whether they find bugs.

Rubrics may consider:

- test design quality;
- alignment with requirements and risks;
- appropriateness of testing scope;
- correctness of implementation;
- reproducibility;
- quality of test data;
- automation quality;
- interpretation of failures;
- documentation and evidence;
- accessibility/security reasoning;
- teamwork and contribution;
- communication of findings.

Where appropriate, instructors may combine team grades with individual reflections, check-ins, contribution evidence, or peer assessment.
