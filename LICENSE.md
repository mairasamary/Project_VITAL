## OpenEMR

OpenEMR is a separate open-source project and is not relicensed by Project VITAL. OpenEMR remains subject to its own licensing terms.

Project VITAL should retrieve or reference OpenEMR rather than vendor the upstream source into this repository unless there is a specific educational reason to maintain a modified course fork.

## Student Work

Student submissions are **not automatically contributed** to Project VITAL and are not automatically relicensed under either license above.

If an instructor wishes to incorporate student-created material into the public kit, appropriate permission should be obtained from the student and the contribution should be separated from grades and other education records.

## Attribution

When reusing Project VITAL materials, attribution should identify:

**Project VITAL — Verification, Integration, Testing, Accessibility & Lifecycle**

and, where practical, link back to the original repository.
