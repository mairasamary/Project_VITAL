# Grading guide (100 points)
Threat model 15: assets/actors/boundaries 5, six threats and STRIDE reasoning 5, diagram and control mapping 5.
Plan 10: category coverage 5, test specificity and expected results 5.
Manual 20: execution/evidence 10, role matrix and positive/negative controls 6, interpretation and cleanup 4.
Scan 10: scoped passive scan 4, three verifications 4, limitations 2.
Findings 10: three evidence-based entries 6, impact/severity and traceability 4.
Regression 15: four meaningful executable checks 8, three live checks and required categories 4, reproducibility and finding linkage 3.
CI 10: three verified runs 6, intended failed assertion 2, restored fixture and additional assertion 2.
AI/reflection 10: five evaluated suggestions 4, implemented and validated suggestion 3, reflection 3.
Award credit for correctly failing tests. Do not count fixture checks as live checks. Inconclusive tests must be identified honestly. Verify final tag, lockfiles where applicable, commands, redaction, and contribution record within relevant criteria; avoid double deductions. Do not require discovery of a vulnerability. Unsafe testing must stop and be referred to instructor.
