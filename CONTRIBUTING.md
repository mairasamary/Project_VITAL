# Contributing to Project VITAL

Thank you for your interest in improving Project VITAL.

Project VITAL is intended to be a reusable, open educational resource for software-testing courses. Contributions from instructors, students, developers, and practitioners are welcome.

## Ways to Contribute

You may contribute by:

- improving assignments or instructor notes;
- adding testing modules;
- correcting technical errors;
- improving accessibility;
- adding reproducible environment configuration;
- adding test-data generation or anonymization tools;
- contributing CI examples;
- documenting adoption experiences;
- proposing alternative Systems Under Test.

## Contribution Expectations

Contributions should:

- be reproducible when practical;
- avoid real patient or protected health information;
- avoid student education records;
- include clear documentation;
- identify any external dependencies;
- respect third-party licenses;
- avoid embedding credentials, API keys, tokens, or secrets.

## Licensing of Contributions

By contributing educational materials, you agree that the contribution may be distributed under **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

By contributing software or code, you agree that the contribution may be distributed under **GPL-3.0-or-later**.

If your contribution includes third-party material, clearly identify its source and license.

## Student Contributions

Student coursework should not be submitted to the public Project VITAL repository by default.

If student-created work is proposed for inclusion, instructors should obtain appropriate permission and ensure the material does not expose grades, feedback, institutional identifiers, or other protected education records.

## Pull Requests

When submitting a pull request:

1. explain the educational or technical purpose;
2. identify the module(s) affected;
3. describe how the contribution was tested;
4. note any new dependencies;
5. confirm that no sensitive or identifying data is included.
